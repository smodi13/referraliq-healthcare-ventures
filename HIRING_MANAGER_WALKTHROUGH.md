# Hiring manager walkthrough (about 90 seconds)

ReferralIQ is an independent candidate work sample. It is not an Intermountain product and has no affiliation with or endorsement
from Intermountain Health or Intermountain Ventures. Private-company numbers are hypothetical.

**The question.** Should a health-system venture group explore equity in a referral and prior authorization AI vendor, run a
controlled commercial pilot, evaluate alternatives, or wait? The commercial and equity decisions are kept separate.

1. **Investment Overview (20 s).** The recommendation is ADVANCE TO DILIGENCE, not invest. The 90-second brief states what is
   evaluated, the lead candidate, why it is only a diligence hypothesis, the economics (hypothetical) and what evidence is missing.
   Each claim carries one of six labels: reported, verified, vendor-reported, analyst judgment, modeled, unverified.
2. **Deal Screener (20 s).** Twelve companies, scored on six dimensions (analyst judgment). Tennr leads on product fit; Silna is the
   first equity diligence candidate. Open the entry-rating sensitivity: Silna is #4 on the Phase 1 score and #6 if its undisclosed
   valuation is not credited as an attractive entry, so its priority is a diligence judgment, not a score lead.
3. **Pilot Economics (25 s).** Base case: the 90-day pilot loses about $39,834 by design, the annual run-rate is about +$25,776, and
   the first scaled year is about -$31,268. Move cash realization from 50% to 30% and run-rate turns negative; break-even is 41.3%.
   Staff capacity value is not cash.
4. **Venture Returns (15 s).** Hypothetical $2M check at $80M pre-money gives 1.9% ownership, 2.97x gross MOIC and 19.9% gross IRR
   over six years in the Base case; the Downside is 0.32x. A 3.0x illustration needs about $80.8M of exit ARR. None of these are
   company facts or an Intermountain hurdle.
5. **Evidence and Gates (10 s).** 36 sources with review status and limitations, and ten diligence gates, all Unknown. The key
   blockers: incumbent overlap (Notable, historical only), verified company metrics, and live entry terms.

**What to inspect first:** the 90-second brief, the Base waterfall on Pilot Economics, and the gates table.
